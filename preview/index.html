About

I am an M.S. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Purdue University, advised by Prof. Younghyun Kim in the NEIS Lab. I work on energy-efficient hardware for machine learning: accelerator architectures, memory-centric designs for LLM inference, and hardware-software co-design.

My recent work, CO-MAC (DAC 2026), is a stochastic multiply-accumulate unit that processes weights in a center-out order, reducing CNN inference latency by up to 54.8% over the state-of-the-art stochastic accelerator. I am now exploring processing-in-memory architectures for memory-bound LLM decoding. I will complete my M.S. in December 2026. Before Purdue, I received my B.S. in Electronic Engineering with highest honors from Hanyang University.
